# Audition General Instructions

> Just let the wardrobe be the character.  You play yourself.
> &mdash;Jack Nicholson

Please exactly follow the "How to audition" directions for the given role.  Upload video auditions to YouTube with [unlisted visibility](https://support.google.com/youtube/answer/157177), and e-mail a link to your video, along with any other requested material, to  **casting.frill392@passinbox.com**.  Please use "audition" as the e-mail subject, and explicitly state in your message which role interests you.  You are welcome to audition for more than one role.  If you have an acting resume or photographs, we welcome you to include those materials too.

For all video auditions, your cellular phone camera and built-in microphone are good enough.  Don't spend a bunch of time on lighting.  We need to assess your suitability for a role; not your ability as a videographer.  Please ensure your camera is stationary and that you are in focus.

If you seem like a good candidate, you will receive a response via e-mail or as a comment on your YouTube video.  If we like your audition but don't select you for a role, we will let you know and contact you directly when we have another part for which you might be a match.
