# Casting FAQ

## Who can audition?

In addition to role-specific requirements, please have reliable transportation and be within communting distance of Raleigh, NC.  You should also be comfortable with basic professional activities: responding to calendar invitations, showing up on time, etc.

## Do I have to be the age listed for a specific role?

Irrespective of your actual age, you should believably be able to play a character in the given age range.

## Will I be paid?

Generally, no.  Nobody, including the core team members, has been paid at this point.  Participation gets your name out there on FilmFreeway.com and in film credits.  We can share footage that you can use to create a reel.  When our films start generating profit, we will be perfectly happy to offer a share to all participants.

We might consider paying you if you already have a non-trivial audience of people willing to pay to see a film in which you have a role.

## Does it matter if I'm a member of the SAG or SAG eligible?

Our productions are strictly non-union at this point.

## What else should I know?

We rehearse!  A lot, if necessary.  Initial readings and rehearsals will occur online via [Signal Desktop](https://signal.org/download/).  When we reach a certain level of proficiency, we will rehearse in-person.

Our current goal is to produce films of steadily increasing quality that could screen at a major festival, like Sundance.  We're not interested in being professional YouTubers.
